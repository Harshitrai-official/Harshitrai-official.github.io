about.md — Harshit Rai

I operate at the intersection of data-driven intelligence and security engineering — a space where models learn attack patterns and machines defend themselves.

My focus is rooted in applied curiosity: identifying how systems fail, exploiting those gaps in controlled environments, and then building smarter defenses. From CTF challenges that sharpen my offensive instincts, to machine learning pipelines that surface hidden anomalies in network traffic — every project is an experiment.

I approach penetration testing methodologically — systematic reconnaissance, service enumeration, exploit selection, and post-exploitation documentation. Every engagement feeds back into better threat modeling and system hardening decisions.

The vision: deploy ML-augmented security tooling that automates routine threat detection, flags behavioral anomalies, and gives security analysts an edge against sophisticated adversaries.
